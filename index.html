      How to book a test

      1. 1

        Choose your tests

        Search the test name or your need (like sugar or thyroid) and tap the test to add it.

      2. 2

        Open My Tests

        Tap the test tube icon. Pick Today or Tomorrow and fill in your details.

      3. 3

        Tap Book Home Collection

        WhatsApp opens with your details. Press Send. Your booking is done when we get your message.

      Slots available. Book for today before 4 PM. If today is full, book for tomorrow. We come to homes within 5 km of the lab.
